Ezra

Ezra is a private, single-user personal assistant. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

Ezra accesses the owner's Google Calendar, Gmail and Google Drive, with the owner's consent, to read and manage the owner's own schedule, email and files.

  • Data is processed only on the owner's own machine, on the owner's behalf.
  • Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • OAuth credentials are stored encrypted locally and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Ezra's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
